About sentiment analysis

FinBERT is a pre-trained NLP model specifically designed for financial sentiment analysis. It classifies each article into one of three classes:

Positive

Optimistic outlook, price increases, favourable market conditions.

Negative

Pessimistic outlook, price drops, unfavourable market conditions.

Neutral

Balanced or factual information without a clear sentiment direction.
